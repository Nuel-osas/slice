Each link opens the swap prefilled with the right pair on Base. You sign in your own wallet. Sizes assume the last oracle print; check the venue quote.

Prices are the Chainlink tokenized-equity feeds on Base: total-return, multiplier-adjusted, 8 decimals, updating on a 0.5% move or a 24 h heartbeat. Holdings use scaledBalanceOf, so one token is never assumed to be one share. Coinbase tokenized stocks are offered under Regulation S in eligible jurisdictions outside the United States; Slice does not offer, sell or execute anything, it only plans and links.
